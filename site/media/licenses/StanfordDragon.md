# Stanford Dragon model source and use terms

The model rendered in the dragon comparison is the Stanford Dragon from the
Stanford Computer Graphics Laboratory's [3D Scanning Repository](https://graphics.stanford.edu/data/3Dscanrep/).
The pinned source archive is
[`dragon_recon.tar.gz`](https://graphics.stanford.edu/pub/3Dscanrep/dragon/dragon_recon.tar.gz),
member `dragon_recon/dragon_vrip.ply`. Cubey's preparation manifest records the
archive, member, source-mesh and prepared-GLB hashes separately.

The repository's terms welcome research use and free mirroring or redistribution.
They permit publishing model images in scholarly articles or books with credit
to the Stanford Computer Graphics Laboratory, and require permission for
commercial use or use in a product for sale. The repository also asks that the
dragon be portrayed respectfully: do not animate or morph it, apply Boolean
operations to it, or depict destructive treatment. The camera moves in Cubey's
recording; the model itself remains intact.

These are the source repository's terms, not a license from Cubey. Consult the
[authoritative repository page](https://graphics.stanford.edu/data/3Dscanrep/)
for the complete and current notice. The site distributes rendered media,
not the source archive or prepared model.
