What to look for

Clamp: clean points, retain structure

The cap acts before accumulation. At 100, isolated white points on the neck, tail and base become much less conspicuous. The horns, layered head bands and broad blue/yellow refraction keep their shape. A cap of 10,000 barely changes the displayed speckles; 1,000 is less effective than 100.

Denoiser: smoother, with a detail cost

OIDN alone sometimes turns intense points into larger pale blobs, especially on the left neck. With the cap, it cleans residual grain and base speckles more successfully. At head-crop scale, it also softens fine ridges and narrow refractive bands. At whole-scene size the benefit is easier to accept.

The cap deliberately loses energy, including legitimate glints. At cap 100, head mean HDR is about 4–5% lower than the matched noisy raw realization. Rectangular crops include background. Two seeds are a practical demo check, not a variance study; the earlier 8192-sample images are also noisy.

What is available now

Renderer: opt-in clamp

--firefly-clamp 100 enables the selected trial. Zero is raw. Checking “Clamp bright samples” in the native panel starts at 100, with an adjustable cap. Changing the cap resets accumulation. Smooth normals stay enabled.

build/dev/projects/raytracing/raytracing \
  --config projects/raytracing/presets/optics/dragon-glass.json \
  --firefly-clamp 100 --samples 512

OIDN: offline evidence

Official OIDN 2.5.1, RT filter, high quality, CUDA, HDR color only. No guides or temporal history. The tool lives in the experiment directory and adds no renderer build dependency.

CLI timings include copies internal to the filter. The process wall time includes startup, file I/O and three executions. These are not native frame-time or motion measurements.

Validation, provenance and limits

The clamp scales each throughput-weighted HDR deposit by one RGB factor when its mean channel value exceeds the cap. It preserves that deposit’s hue. Primary visible atmosphere is excluded. Geometry, sun and material recipes retain their hashes.

Clamp-off PNGs reproduce the earlier captures: three are pixel-identical; one differs by one code value in one channel of one pixel. HDR arithmetic is not bit-identical after the shader change. All 16 renders are finite, nonnegative and validation-clean; positive transport-error flags are zero and flag counts are identical across caps. Zero-weight termination is retained separately.

The offline display transform was calibrated against every renderer PNG: maximum error one RGB8 code value, mean under 0.047. Denoised images use that same calibrated transform. No high-sample capture is treated as ground truth.

Native Vulkan UI check on a private Xvfb display: raw → cap 100 → raw, three history epochs, saved cap values 0 / 100 / 0. No temporal denoising or physical-display acceptance is claimed. Full receipts and analysis.
