What the flux calculation measures

Output radiance is integrated against projected geometric solid angle on both sides. For each constant-normal piece, reflection has unit solid-angle Jacobian; Snell transmission contributes |w·N| / (IOR² |v·N|). The camera radiance factor cancels the IOR² term. The geometric control gives one pointwise. Fallback can select multiple preimages or none.

The local reduced-radiance furnace identity still passes to 1.19 × 10⁻⁷. Complementary Fresnel weights alone do not prove this mapping conserves flux. No complete closed-body furnace or full-scene energy qualification is claimed.

Critical-angle and experimental limits
