## Decision

Keep the qualified local-cache and eight-sphere building blocks. A tiny edit can
skip most old field evaluation and preserve retained brick slots. Cached plus
eight same-operation sphere intervals is a useful fast preview. Four/four prefix
publication, failure preservation and settled original-Cached output pass the
serialized native checks.

Stop the joint live-service experiment at its measured cost gate. Four small
Guardian cuts need about **10.6 ms GPU whole**, before CPU preparation, while
four frames at 2 ms maintenance per frame provide only 8 ms. Slicing bounds units
but raises total cost: about 16 ms GPU at 512 groups, 37.7 ms at 128 groups and
132.4 ms at 32 groups. The 128-group maximum unit wall cost is about 1.98 ms in
the measured series; its complete job still cannot sustain the proposed stream.
The unchanged 8-slot queue supplies temporary headroom, not service capacity.

Robot has substantially cheaper construction and remains promising. This loop
does not qualify Robot's paced input service or silently substitute its result
for Guardian. Interleaved full-frame deadlines, timestamped input, live bindings,
delayed old readers, and large-edit 4/8/16-frame completion remain unqualified.
The conditional S1/S2 work stops before claiming those outcomes. No pending-cap
increase, quality relaxation, shared allocator or source-capacity change is used
to hide the miss.

## Locality and exactness

The builder starts with a bit-preserving raw clone of the old node buffer and
atlas. It skips reachable subtrees using a guarded bound derived from the
active-brick sample enclosure. Unchanged slots retain their pointers. Dirty old
samples apply the admitted hard operation at the original lattice positions;
new or numerically ambiguous samples use canonical evaluation. Locality reduces
evaluation and sample writes; this first storage route still copies the whole
329 MiB candidate payload.

Uniform-to-subdivided changes initialize all 64 exposed children, including
children outside the brush band. Descendants below old uniform ancestors are
not trusted. Retired subtrees can leave unreachable bytes; later exposures
initialize them. Remove/recreate chains and slot holes are explicitly covered.

Active count and allocated-slot high-water are separate. New slots append to a
bounded range; removed slots are not reclaimed. One helper owns the metadata
throughout its private local chain. Passing an unknown noncompact field between
helpers is outside the study contract. The native hosts retain at most two
complete Fields and reject capacity overflow while preserving the old display.

The separately qualified root-operand shortcut evaluates the old source at a
new brick's center. A strict guarded `2R` separation can prove the tool wins
throughout the brick. Tool-only sampling is then exact for that region; uncertain
bricks retain full source sampling. This is a narrow whole-brick certificate.
The general regional evaluator rejected by the previous loop remains excluded.

Both local routes pass 39 standalone and 32 chained fresh-cache pairs. Native
comparisons normalize logical topology and physical addressing, then compare
every active raw R16 word. Retained slots never move in this roster. Multi-stamp
and sliced/calibrated candidates receive separate canonical comparisons and
independent saved-dump checks. Numerical guards are engineering bounds qualified
on these cases, not a universal IEEE proof or arbitrary-modifier guarantee.

## Eight-sphere preview and prefix accounting

The private preview sorts and merges sphere intervals, then uses the existing
Cached walk outside them. It retains one syntactic base-walk call site. Tools
determine occupancy inside their intervals; endpoint ownership selects analytic
sphere or workspace-face normals. Additions are workspace clipped; subtraction
keeps the cutter raw. Base normals and Cached traversal stay unchanged.

82 regular geometry rays pass the frozen Cached controls, with maximum depth
error 0.0002514934 against a 0.0002790179 limit and maximum normal error 0.381°
against 2°. All 49 pending/base ownership checks pass. Disjoint/overlapping
stamps, starts inside, hidden base boundaries, equal/touching endpoints,
workspace edges and regular grazing rays are covered. Exact tangency and
sub-grid controls remain separate diagnostics. Subject camera rays and their
corresponding shadow replays complete without tracer exhaustion.

Admission accepts 0/1/2/4/8 pending spheres and rejects the tested ninth stamp,
mixed operations, changed base/palette/radius/scale/material, smoothing,
non-sphere tools and inconsistent cut-material settings. The fixed opaque
brush/palette and same-operation stroke are MVP study restrictions. Source,
history and materials remain canonical and separate from the preview controls.

28 serialized sequences cover each subject/operation with normal publication
and six failure/retry dispositions. A prefix of four is baked while the last
four remain visible. Intermediate images match an independent fresh prefix
cache plus the same tail; final images match the original Cached executable.
Every rendered ID appears exactly once. Retained trees and histories match
their inputs; prefix IDs retire only after replacement-frame completion.
Four serialized retained-source files also replay through the original public
Cached executable and produce byte-identical settled images.

Allocation refusal and cancellation are decision injections. Frontier and
append tests force real GPU capacity exceptions using a lowered private
candidate limit. Stale/discarded candidates complete work and then refuse
publication. Failed attempts retain byte-identical preview frames and all eight
pending IDs. Descriptor updates happen only after frame/readback completion;
these are not concurrent-input or in-flight lifetime tests.

## Performance interpretation

Whole raw clones take about 1.28 ms GPU and 1.4 ms wall. Sixteen MiB slabs total
about .93 ms GPU with a maximum transfer near .08 ms, but serial per-unit waits
increase clone wall cost to about 2.5–2.7 ms. This floor fits further experiments;
a persistent journal/shared atlas is not the first extra mechanism justified
by these measurements.

The long preview series compares original Cached, new zero, original one-edit
and new 1/2/4/8 routes at 1080p SS1, preset/close views and existing shadows and
lighting. Three interleaved repeats retain 30 warmup and 150 measured frames
each. Eight pending stamps stay around 2–3 ms GPU Guardian, 4 ms Robot preset
and 8 ms Robot close. These are scene GPU times, not full-frame cadence or
physical input latency. Edited geometry can reduce base-walk work, so these
placements do not establish a universal per-stamp overhead.

Construction compares full canonical, prior global reuse, local and local plus
dominance at radii .02/.065/.15. Each final row has three interleaved repeats,
three warmups and ten measured commits. Candidate Fields are already allocated;
complete wall time includes helper admission and waits. Rendering does not run
alongside these jobs. Unit calibration separately compares whole dispatches and
32/128/512/2048 groups for four radius-.025 stamps, with the same repetitions.

At the previous radius-.065 placement, pooled median complete wall times are:

| Subject / operation | Full canonical ms | Global reuse ms | Local ms | Local + dominance ms |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Guardian add | 995.676 | 111.121 | 75.617 | 24.433 |
| Guardian cut | 951.030 | 85.049 | 50.920 | 30.718 |
| Robot add | 99.217 | 27.373 | 8.297 | 4.335 |
| Robot cut | 95.437 | 24.571 | 5.990 | 4.177 |

Guardian local + dominance spends about 15.3 ms on repeated helper admission,
6.3–6.4 ms classification GPU and 1.28 ms raw copy GPU. Canonical sampling falls
to zero for add and about 6.27 ms for cut. Robot's corresponding complete
construction is about 4.2–4.3 ms. Initial parsing, pipeline setup and simultaneous
rendering are outside these commit rows.

Smaller work units retain identical SPIR-V and raw-cache output. Their cost is
not linear in brick count: repeating small dispatches greatly increases total
Guardian cut time. Canonical fallback rises from about 3.25 ms whole to
119.7 ms at 32 groups; classification rises from about 6.07 to 11.47 ms.
The shader still evaluates a long canonical program during
classification and fallback. CPU revalidation also remains part of construction
age; it cannot be subtracted from a live latency claim without a qualified
preparation path.

## Next decision

The [interactive editing followup plan](sdf-interactive-edit-followup-plan.md)
separates Guardian's commit-service probe from varying-radius rounded brush
segments. Guardian already uses 996 tapered segments; new brush support does
not simplify that existing source. Segments provide continuity; spatial
path/width admission must supply any reduction in incoming edit count. The
larger maintenance-budget test preserves the exact cache contract; approximate
construction remains a separate future fork.

The [completed followup](sdf-interactive-edit-results.md) qualifies tapered
preview/local commits and measures path/width admission savings. Guardian still
misses its paced service target; repeated source preparation and scheduling
utilization are the next scoped questions.

Investigate reuse or amortization of exact canonical node-center evaluations,
and compare explicit admission rates or larger maintenance allowances. Keep raw
cache identity as the oracle. A narrower source-evaluation optimization must
pass independently; do not reintroduce the rejected general regional sampler.

Then qualify bounded worker preparation, interleaved rendering, immutable
frame/version bindings, actual old-reader retirement, timestamped arrivals,
admission pauses and drain rate. A precompiled script can be a control but does
not establish live source-preparation latency. Eight pending slots still count
the frozen prefix, and a full queue must pause admission without dropping accepted
geometry.

All public modes/defaults, the preserved public executable and 211 original
SPIR-V files remain unchanged. Canonical source limits remain 1024 leaves,
2048 instructions, depth 24 and stack 32; a finite admitted stream is not a
long-session capacity result. Ordinary Cached sub-grid/grazing limitations and
the earlier unscored Guardian depth pixel remain separate. No brush/undo UI,
public promotion, push, project-site deployment or XR acceptance is included.
