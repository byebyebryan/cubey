## What the presentation shows

The observatory leads the SDF landing page and README panel. Each model has a
native poster, recorded film, detail route and local static/build/film preset.
The robot film visits all 75 saved actions. The three architectural films are
camera arcs around completed static scenes; their wheels and water do not move.
The observatory's intact and section views share every capture setting except
the scene binding. That comparison exposes the telescope, mount and keeper room
without presenting another demo or claiming live Boolean interaction.

The [construction chapter](../../site/src/pages/demos/sdf/inside-a-solid.astro)
connects the robot's saved build, the observatory section, distance caching,
rendering choices and material cuts. Native media, explanatory diagrams and
saved construction have distinct captions. Presentation captures explicitly use
Balanced and SS2; the application default remains Cached. Recorded 30 FPS is
an encoding choice, not an interactive frame-time or editing-latency result.

The site build selects the quartet and retains historical media. Guardian's old
route resolves to its retirement notice. Publication is checked against the
served revision and asset identities, separately from native qualification.

## Supported native surface

The [SDF guide](../../projects/sdf/README.md) is the operational entry point.
The [tracked roster](../../projects/sdf/tools/csg_showcase.json) pins the accepted
scene hashes and current presets. The renderer retains authored primitives and
operations, named assemblies/materials/history, saved seeking/replay, export,
the shared workshop, Cached default and explicit higher-detail modes.
No runtime, shader, material or model change is introduced by this presentation.

The portable capture command reads tracked JSONs directly, freezes its inputs
and records commands, source revision, binary/shader identities, logs and actual
decoded frame counts. It requires a qualified native build, Vulkan validation
and FFmpeg/ffprobe; it does not require an earlier draft report. The
[web media guide](../../site/media/README.md) describes optional web encoding
and ledger import. Normal site builds use the committed web assets without
requiring the native capture tree or FFmpeg.

## Research boundaries

Public Cached-mode geometry edits still rebuild the full field synchronously.
The later private experiments qualify canonical local-cache updates, pending
preview of up to eight rounded tapered primitives, immutable preparation and
finite timestamped input on a simple scene. They do not provide a public brush
editor or a guarantee for indefinite editing. The retained
[service results](sdf-edit-service-results.md) record 27/28 strict batch trials,
including Guardian's 71.23 ms miss, and 18/18 finite simple-scene trials.
Guardian's 1,011/1,024 leaves remain evidence of source-growth limits despite
its retirement from presentation.

Reopen continuous editing only for a concrete constrained use case, with source
growth/compaction, grouped live-stroke undo and reader lifetime addressed.
General editing controls, smoothing/domain expansion and sustained complex-scene
editing remain deferred. VR is not a current target.

The separate horse mesh-to-field feasibility work explores an offline converted
mesh, rather than a primitive reconstruction. It remains a separate uncommitted
work unit. Native import, storage/normal attributes, GI, transmission and scene
lighting require their own design and acceptance pass. CSG remains a supported
geometry source; the approved quartet can serve as fixed rendering subjects.
