# Authored CSG milestone closeout

Date: 2026-11-04. Status: complete locally; public deployment unchanged.

The active CSG presentation is the **field-kit robot**, saved construction
playback, and the shared **material workshop**. Guardian is retired to
[studies](../../studies/sdf/guardian/README.md); its current artistic review
supersedes the earlier selected-sculpture status. Earlier blockouts, dioramas
and organic auditions remain experiments, not additional selected demos.

A subsequent [four-scene form refinement](sdf-csg-form-refinement.md) prepares
the robot and three architectural dioramas as showcase candidates, after the
user reopened that art selection. It retains this closeout's original sources
and evidence. Public media selection and deployment are unchanged.

## What remains supported

The SDF project retains its authored primitive/operation vocabulary, named
assemblies/materials/history, saved seeking/replay, export, workshop, Cached
default and explicit higher-detail tracing modes. Shared geometry, materials,
renderer and shaders are unchanged. The robot's geometry/presets remain intact.

The public native entry points are documented in the [SDF guide](../../projects/sdf/README.md).
The [construction chapter](../../site/src/pages/demos/sdf/inside-a-solid.astro)
continues to explain solids, cuts, cached distance queries and materials through
the robot and specimens. Guardian is unlisted; its old site route is a small
retirement notice. Historical media/credits are retained.

## What the editing research established

The later private experiments qualify local canonical-cache updates, pending
preview of up to eight rounded tapered primitives, immutable preparation and
finite timestamped input on a simple scene. They do not provide a public brush
editor or a guarantee for indefinite editing.

The retained [bounded service results](sdf-edit-service-results.md) record 27/28
strict batch trials, including Guardian's 71.23 ms miss. The finite simple-scene
route passed 18/18 trials. These dated workloads are not rerun or expanded by
this closeout. Guardian's 1,011/1,024 leaves expose source-growth limits; removing
it from the showcase does not remove that technical result.

Sustained complex-scene editing, source growth/compaction, grouped live-stroke
undo, multiple in-flight reader retirement and a general editing UI are deferred
research. Smoothing/domain expansion is not an active closeout requirement.
No VR target is retained.

## Next exploration

The separate [horse feasibility report](http://starship:8787/reports/dad7ee569b5ca7bf507b6a924f0ac7ea50f3d8b6a391286944bfbf1551a580f2/)
records an offline mesh-to-field prototype. It does not replace CSG source with a primitive reconstruction and

shading-normal attributes and native opaque rendering require their own design
pass before implementation. CSG remains one supported geometry source.

tests, 20 active entries, 13 native owners and 21 built pages. Active pages do
not promote Guardian; the old route resolves to its retirement notice.

The broad documentation CLI target cannot pass on this SDF-focused build because
unrelated application binaries are absent. No unrelated rebuild was performed.

acceptance remains unverified. Built URLs/media and native captures were checked

One legacy migration script ignored `--help` and rewrote its output during a
probe. Fingerprint verification detected it; original scene bytes were restored,
only preset bindings reapplied, argparse help added, and preservation repeated.
No regenerated scene entered the final retirement.
