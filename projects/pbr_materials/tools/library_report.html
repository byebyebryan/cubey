Frosting changes the transmitted image.

Both reflection and transmission soften as roughness increases. These pairs keep the camera, shape, IOR and absorption fixed against a generated sharp-bar environment. The middle setting is the library default.

Mesh uses the existing forward volume approximation. SDF filters environment radiance along its bounded interface path and measures in-medium distances for absorption. It does not transmit arbitrary opaque scene objects. The two transport methods share material intent; their images are not expected to match.

Look at the ingredients.

The rust coverage field changes metalness and roughness as well as color. White in the metalness view is steel; dark regions are dielectric oxide.

Who owns a cut surface?

A cut can inherit the surrounding procedural rust or deliberately expose a plain steel companion material. This is an explicit material assignment, rather than an automatic weathering simulation.

What happens when the weave gets smaller?

Fine relief fades as it becomes unresolved while the material retains a dry base and restrained sheen. The weave follows explicit material X/Y axes; the swatch avoids promising seamless cloth draping over arbitrary shapes.

Kraft is a stylized directional-fiber surface. This pass does not model corrugated layers, torn edges or loose fibers. Wood likewise aims for a readable solid grain field, without species accuracy, knots or figured-wood scattering.
