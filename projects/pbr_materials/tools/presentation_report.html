01 / PBR materials

Let the surfaces differ.

Wood grain, dry woven yarns, steel under oxide and satin bronze share a frozen low-sun environment. A small camera arc reveals their different structure and response to light.

Frosted glass was less legible in this group, so bronze takes its place. The full library retains focused clear/frosted comparisons with a detailed background.

02 / SDF materials

The material has an inside.

The block and its through-cut are saved implicit operations. When the cut is committed, its new interior samples the same object-anchored wood field. The grain is evaluated there without a separate image or UV layout.

Inspect the operation.

Native snapshots at the same camera position. Select a saved state to see what the operation changes.

A mesh can represent this finished shape too. This study makes the implicit construction and the material coordinates across newly exposed surfaces inspectable. Continuous brush sculpting is a separate experiment.
