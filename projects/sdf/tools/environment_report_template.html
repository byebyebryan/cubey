What changed

One shared environment

Visible sky, sun direction/color, diffuse response, prefiltered reflections and glass lookups come from the shared atmosphere runtime. Previous/current reflection probes blend as complete captures. A settled scene reuses its probes while the camera moves.

A distant planet contributes lower-hemisphere reflection radiance; no ground plane or terrain is rendered. The visible backdrop uses the shared presentation horizon policy.

Bounded cloud integration

Shared broken cumulus supplies the visible layer and reflected/refracted environment. Both use frozen weather at 300 s, coverage 0.28 and the same low sun. Foreground depth excludes the sculpture from background composition, including glass.

128-pixel cube faces, five roughness levels, 64-step cloud probes; half-resolution, 64-step visible clouds. No animated weather or new cloud renderer.
