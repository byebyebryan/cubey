Four deliberately simple new looks.

Dry clay

Earth color and roughness 0.90 give broad, matte highlights. This is a material preset; the old gray “clay” switch remains a neutral inspection override at roughness 0.36.

Glazed ceramic

A smooth teal dielectric response gives a simple glaze-like finish. It adds no crackle, layered coating or porcelain scattering.

Satin bronze

Metallic 1 removes the diffuse body contribution and gives the reflections their warm color. There is no brushing, oxidation or added surface decoration.

The first opaque audition was readable, so its parameters were retained. No new noise patterns or extra realism passes were added. The stone presets are references, not another marble revision.

Glass: useful result, bounded claim.

The sphere qualifies with the fixed homogeneous model. Refraction follows the interfaces, and measured path length controls tint. The shader processes at most four crossings, sampling the environment for reflection and the final transmitted direction.

Total internal reflection terminates with an environment approximation, rather than following more internal bounces. It affects a substantial portion of the guardian and is reported separately. This slice has no caustics, dispersion, frosted transmission, nested media or opaque objects seen through glass. Those limits were retained instead of expanding the experiment.
