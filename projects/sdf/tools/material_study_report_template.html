The three changes.

Neutral reference

The inherited gray-green surface becomes neutral gray. Uniform roughness 0.76 and no pattern keep it useful as a normally lit baseline.

Ivory ceramic

Warm ivory and roughness 0.34 soften the original teal glaze and distinguish it from bronze. The material stays a simple dielectric.

Granular terracotta

Fine model-space bump adds surface grain without color stains. Specular roughness is near 1.0; a separate rough diffuse response reduces the smooth, waxy shading.

What made the clay look shiny?

The old surface's broad bright areas remain in its diffuse-only view. Its reflection-only view is much darker, and the roughness diagnostic confirms 0.90 reaches shading. Raising specular roughness or removing reflection alone cannot explain that smooth appearance.

The first grain trial was too strong and pebbly. The corrective pass keeps finer, shallower grain and adds an opt-in Oren–Nayar direct diffuse response. Environment diffuse uses a conservative A-only approximation with the existing irradiance cube; this is a bounded rough-surface look, not fully integrated rough diffuse transport. The color remains uniform. Native visual review does not substitute for your preference.

Glass: useful result, bounded claim.

The sphere qualifies with the fixed homogeneous model. Refraction follows the interfaces, and measured path length controls tint. The shader processes at most four crossings, sampling the environment for reflection and the final transmitted direction.

Total internal reflection terminates with an environment approximation, rather than following more internal bounces. It affects a substantial portion of the guardian and is reported separately. This slice has no caustics, dispersion, frosted transmission, nested media or opaque objects seen through glass. Those limits were retained instead of expanding the experiment.
