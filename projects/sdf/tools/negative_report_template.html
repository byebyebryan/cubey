__ORACLE__

Replay images are not bit identical: rebuilding the same input also produces tiny sampled-image differences. Recipe hashes match, and GPU rays pass the unchanged geometric tolerance. Separately recorded 8-bit image limits are max 16 code values, mean 0.002, and at most 0.01% of channels differing by more than one; base/cue and 30/60 FPS cue comparisons require max one. These limits do not relax the ray oracle.
