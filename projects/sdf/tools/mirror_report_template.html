Original and mirrored

The mirrored variant reflects the original left-side strokes, including horn shape and projected carving, onto the right. Central and cross-face forms retain their authored asymmetry. It is a separate scene; the original remains unchanged.

What mirroring saves

Pairing reduces 113 separate stroke placements to 65 actions, a 42.5% reduction. Grouping curve segments into those strokes reduces the old 1,012-state construction to 66 states. This implementation expands ordinary primitives on both sides: it saves authoring and replay work, while the final rendering still evaluates the full model.
