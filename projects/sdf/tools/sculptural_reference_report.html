The rejected drafts lacked a convincing body structure. This shortlist looks for actual proportions, useful surface transitions and readable contact between parts. These are external references, not new Cubey renders.

My recommendation: start with the recumbent tiger. It has five museum views and directly tests the body, head and paw relationships that failed in the sleeper.

The strongest fantasy direction is Zhelong Xu’s Tomb Guardian. It offers a much richer face and silhouette, with a correspondingly larger modeling task. Pompon’s owl is a compact alternative; Quilez’s Snail is a later SDF scene benchmark. These are proposals for review, not selected implementation.

__CARDS__

Read the images at small size first, then open the original sources. A successful plain form still needs secondary structure: cheek pads, a brow ridge, a folded limb or a shell lip. Removing fine texture should preserve those landmarks.

The photographs are perspective references, not calibrated orthographic views. Matching should be judged visually, with mismatches made explicit.

Existing primitives and local Boolean blends are plausible tools for this study. That is an authoring hypothesis, not a demonstrated fidelity result. No geometry, renderer, editing UI or performance implementation was undertaken in this research pass.

Dreams and Quilez remain relevant, but “organic” needs a more precise brief than softness or irregularity. These supporting sources help distinguish the shape work from the finished surface treatment.

    __METHODS__
Sources, access limits and all reference views

Museum object identities and dates were checked against collection records. Reference photographs were inspected directly. The Tomb Guardian page was indexed but blocked direct fetching; one creator-CDN front image was inspected. Snail’s live shader was inaccessible; a credited preview was inspected and its SDF attribution corroborated by primary graphics research. No live shader execution or video viewing is claimed.

Embedded images retain their own credits and rights. Reference use does not make an external artwork a Cubey asset or grant a source-code license. The public-domain museum images and CC BY owl photograph have explicit source links. The two contemporary artwork previews are included for commentary.
