Start with the sculpted form.

Three native clay drafts exploring gesture, continuous masses and carved space. A reset after the regular, mechanical dioramas, guided by your original interest in Dreams and Inigo Quilez’s sculptural work.

Coiled sleeperForest spiritEroded refuge

Choose the shape before the detail. The same plain clay and lighting keep attention on silhouette and how the volumes meet. Each subject has another angle and an uncarved state. These are early form studies; none is selected as the next finished scene.

__PANELS__

All three use the existing native SDF renderer. Smooth unions fuse broad masses; tapered strokes carry the gestures; subtraction establishes eyelids, hollows and chambers. The difference we are judging here is the authored shape. Organic forms are a useful direction for this demo, rather than something exclusive to SDF rendering.

Native sources, full-size images and validation

Nine native stills at 1440×1080, Balanced with 2× supersampling, all with required Vulkan validation. Six authored scenes and presets use the existing runtime and shaders. The buttons switch captured images; “Before carving” is a separate authored modeling state, not an interactive editing demonstration.

Some curve joins and shading transitions remain visible. A separate Analytical refuge control retained similar broad transitions; these drafts do not establish a renderer diagnosis or a performance result.
