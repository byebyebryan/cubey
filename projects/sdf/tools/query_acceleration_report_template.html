Do not select as a winner

How the timing result was qualified

Three interleaved serial repetitions of all four stepping controls, A and A+B, all three Guardian and Robot views. Each run has 180 frames, 30 warmup and 150 measured samples. Every GPU pass contains frame indices 30–179. The archive reproduces every p95 from 64,800 GPU pass samples. No owned build, test, capture or other GPU job overlapped the timed batch.

Useful gain: at least 0.5 ms and 5% in a Guardian view, beyond control variation. Regression gate: a view must not regress by both over 0.5 ms and 3%. Shared encoder activity has means of 32.3–33.0% across controls. Driver: 610.57.04.

02 / Why the predictor helped

There are enough exact queries to learn from

Guardian averages about nine consecutive exact primary queries between cached advances. Adaptive prediction uses accepted field samples to choose a larger trial step; fixed relaxation guesses the same factor everywhere.

Preserved contracts

  • Cached certificates remain traversal bounds; they do not train the predictor as exact field values.
  • Each trial performs its own region lookup. Coverage is checked before accepting a hit.
  • Inside exits, root/ray endpoints and the shared 4,096-iteration budget remain.
  • The original six-query normal stencil and all secondary tracers remain intact.

04 / The other opportunity

Finer pruning is a construction problem now

The pilot refines 256 parents sampled by program length and spatial octant, including 225 with smooth operators. It builds 16,384 children and passes 245,760 independent center, corner and normal-stencil checks. These are CPU pilot observations.

A64 has 13,058 active cells out of 262,144. Refining only those cells can avoid dense 256³ headers. Pruning could benefit primary, normal and shadow queries, but the forecast construction cost does not fit the proposed edit-latency gate.

Forecast, not a full-level result. These stratum-weighted point estimates have no confidence interval. No full sparse level was constructed or GPU-benchmarked. The pilot does not qualify a view-hot partial hierarchy. Partial refinement, faster construction or partial updates remain separate possible experiments.
