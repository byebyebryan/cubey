__FEATURES__

1 / Site and architecture

Fractured rock planes and ledges, offset retaining courses, a supported stair, arched windows, entrance stones, paneled doors, shutters, a clock and roof vane.

2 / Dome and instrument

Circular dome seams, rivets, turntable teeth and brackets. A hollow telescope tube with recessed objective, collars, eyepiece, fork mount, focus wheel and counterweight.

3 / A place to work

Timber floors, observing logs, eyepiece cabinet, chart desk, rolled map, stool and ink pot. More planting and instrument supports complete the terraces.

From the selected draft to the detail passes.

The same hero camera, sun and exposure make the changes easier to compare. The original is a retained 1280×960 render; the new captures are 1600×1200. These buttons select stills from the authored passes.

The site remains a stylized miniature, with regular masonry and simple materials. The section is an authored Boolean variant; animated cutting, construction playback and interactive performance were not qualified in this content pass. The current modeling result awaits your visual review.

Native recipes and evidence

All eight new stills rendered with required Vulkan validation, the existing Balanced renderer and 2× supersampling. Runtime code and shaders were preserved. Named history is saved; these stages are modeling iterations rather than a captured construction film.
