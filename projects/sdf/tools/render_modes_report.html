Sampling is part of the real default experience

The audit found SS2 in 91 of 95 presets. It renders four times as many internal pixels, but measured costs scale differently by scene. The 96-run sampling study therefore compared Cached, refined Cached, Balanced and the earlier analytical A+B endpoint at both sample settings before changing the mode default. Every existing preset's sample setting is preserved.

Balanced/SS1 is a useful lower-cost alternative. Selecting it is an explicit sampling choice; the Rendering selector itself does not lower resolution, change shadows or discard material edits.

Ordinary native controls

The native tests exercise actual menu selection, both sample settings, resize/recreate, orbit/zoom/reset, normal debug and return, rejected/retried owned reload, material editing and authored history seek/undo/redo/playback. Source-only Analytical holds no field allocation. Switching back constructs the field when needed; cached-to-cached and sample-only changes reuse it.

Analytical needed its existing accelerator

The initial public mapping retained ordinary A16 pruning and exposed Guardian costs around 135–492 ms in the completed investigation runs. That batch was stopped and excluded from final acceptance. The existing A64 root-far accelerator is now qualified for the public atmosphere Analytical route, including native-window history, resize, appearance changes and failed/retried reload. It reuses compatible region metadata with Balanced.

Material/optical analytical rendering and Hybrid retain their existing A16 backend. Prepared-cone and adaptive-step experiments remain explicit private studies. The public Analytical values here measure A64 root-far with ordinary stepping and automatic shadow specialization, not the earlier prepared A+B study endpoint.

Frames, switching and edits are different costs

Geometry publication waits for completed GPU work after a real full-scene primitive edit. Cached geometry still rebuilds synchronously. The default change does not make continuous sculpting responsive. A first observed material-workshop pipeline setup took 11.45 seconds; a repeated setup took 8.30 ms. These are process observations without deliberately clearing the driver cache. First selection, warm pipeline creation, field regeneration and GPU frame cost are separate costs.

Acceptance and limits

    Twenty named-mode/low-level pairs across both subjects and sample settings are pixel-identical. The five-mode history matrix has 45 action frames. Forty native feature captures cover AO, soft shadows, composition, material families, glass/clouds, lighting cycles, playback and debug views. Frozen source contracts pass on the unprepared root-far route. The existing cached-cell and prior quality evidence are reused through unchanged shader/source identities.

    All native GPU jobs run serially. No owned builds, tests, captures or encoding overlap final timing/publication. Boundary load observations and full raw samples remain in the receipts; they do not prove continuous host exclusivity. Native virtual-window qualification is separate from physical desktop and human visual approval.
