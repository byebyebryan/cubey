The guardian has one material, so shaded hits can read it directly instead of walking the entire CSG program to find its owner. The plain and procedural rows above use that shortcut. Pattern and bump differences are smaller than this run's timing variation; they do not imply a rendering speedup.

Field construction remains synchronous at about 0.92 seconds for the finished model. Material edits preserve that field. Live construction can still skip operations while rebuilding; the offline film visits every operation.

Checks and limits

Both new scenes preserve the original geometry and history. Geometric-normal and distance images are exact matches to the plain scene. Repeated renders, the one-material shortcut, disabled-pattern controls and export/reload pass native comparisons; seed edits change appearance without rebuilding distance bricks. A separate mixed-material fixture checks smooth blends and explicit cut materials.

Desktop checks cover material editing, pattern/bump controls, and mirrored stroke seeking/undo/redo. Native images and film frames were inspected. Browser interaction review was unavailable because no browser was connected.

This is an opaque surface approximation. Specular sky reflections, subsurface scattering, true grain displacement, weathering layers and per-part material frames remain future work. The pattern uses fixed scene/model coordinates; it follows neither changing reveal bounds nor individual moving pieces.
