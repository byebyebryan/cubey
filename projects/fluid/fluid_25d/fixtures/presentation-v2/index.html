How to read it

Uniform rain supplies the whole terrain. Narrow streams follow the existing slopes and feed the broad collection. The opening is truly dry; the final water state holds while the camera, rain, foam and solar clock continue moving.

Screen time: 0.4 s dry opening → 5.6 s accelerated formation → 2 s animated final-state hold. The water spans approximately six simulated hours; afternoon-to-sunset lighting is an independent artistic clock, not a geographic storm reconstruction.
