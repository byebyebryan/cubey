# Retained Fluid 2.5D study harnesses

These are dated research, comparison and reproduction tools, not the supported
Terrain Water startup path. Their tests remain registered; moving a harness does
not requalify its original results. Outputs and optional environments are not
repository dependencies. Use [the study index](../README.md) first.

The two frozen narrative snapshots are preserved byte-for-byte:
[README history](README_HISTORY.md) and [presentation history](presentation_history.md).
Historical source paths in those snapshots refer to their recorded revision;
use the [relocation map](../../relocations.json) to locate retained files today.
